Privacy Policy

MUST Alumni Portal

Last Updated: February 25, 2026

Welcome to the MUST Alumni Portal ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Important: By using the MUST Alumni Portal app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our app.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you register and use our app, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, graduation year, course/program, and profile photo
  • Authentication Data: Login credentials, Google Sign-In information
  • Profile Details: Bio, skills, interests, employment information, and location
  • Communication Data: Messages, chat history, story reactions, and replies

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, time spent on app, interaction patterns
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address (if permitted)
  • Log Data: Error logs, crash reports, and performance data

1.3 User-Generated Content

  • Stories (images and text)
  • Event posts and RSVPs
  • Articles and career tips
  • Job opportunities posted
  • Comments and reactions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: Create and manage your account, authenticate users
  • Communication: Enable messaging, notifications, and story interactions between alumni
  • Content Delivery: Display personalized content, events, news, and opportunities
  • Networking: Connect alumni based on shared interests, courses, and graduation years
  • Notifications: Send push notifications for messages, events, and important updates
  • Analytics: Improve app performance, understand user behavior, and enhance features
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 With Other Users

Your profile information, stories, posts, and public activities are visible to other verified MUST alumni users within the app.

3.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, cloud storage, and push notifications
  • Cloudinary: Image hosting and optimization
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication services

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental request, or to protect our rights and safety.

3.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Data is stored securely using Firebase Cloud Firestore with encryption
  • Secure authentication using Firebase Authentication
  • HTTPS encryption for data transmission
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication requirements
Note: While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Update

You can access and update your profile information at any time through the app settings.

5.2 Delete Account

You may request deletion of your account by contacting us. Upon deletion, your personal data will be removed, though some information may be retained for legal or legitimate business purposes.

5.3 Notification Preferences

You can manage push notification settings through your device settings or app preferences.

5.4 Data Portability

You may request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used format.

5.5 Opt-Out

You can opt out of certain data collection by adjusting your device permissions or app settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for alumni who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. We may retain certain information after account deletion for:

  • Legal compliance and dispute resolution
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Backup and disaster recovery (up to 90 days)
  • Aggregated analytics (anonymized data)

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an in-app notification for significant changes

Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Third-Party Links

Our app may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
