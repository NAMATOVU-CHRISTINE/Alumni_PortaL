🎓 Delete Your Alumni Portal Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you wish to delete your Alumni Portal account and all associated data, please follow the instructions below.

What will be deleted:
  • Your profile information (name, email, graduation year)

To request account deletion:

Send an email to our support team with your account details. Please include your registered email address in the request.
